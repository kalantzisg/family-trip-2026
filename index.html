🚆 Getting to Marlin Apartments (London Bridge):

Fri 26 – Tue 30 Sep
Peloponnisos Exploration — 5 Days · Coastal & Scenic
Peloponnese
🏠 Chalandri base note: The Chalandri maisonette booking runs continuously. Drive the loop with overnight bags only — leave the rest at Chalandri. Keep the base: security and convenience outweighs any saving for a group of 5.

Since Nafplio was covered on a previous trip, this loop drives to the furthest south, bases there for beach days, then works back north via the stunning eastern coast to Monemvasia (beachside overnight) then Xylokastro to see a family friend.

🚗 Day 1 — Fri 26 Sep · Chalandri → Stoupa (Deep Mani base)
Drive time: ~3h 30m · ~280km · via Corinth motorway → Kalamata → coastal mountain road
Route note: Good fast motorway to Kalamata (toll ~), then a winding but spectacular mountain road down to the coast. The road from Kalamata to Stoupa is very windy — hairpin bends with views that are literally to die for, and the descent towards Kardamyli yields stunning views along the coast. Take it easy — this is not the road to rush.

Base: Stoupa or Kardamyli — 2 nights here.
Stoupa has the best sandy beach in the area and is the closest you'll get to a holiday resort in the Mani — a seafront strip of tourist bars and restaurants. Kardamyli is a pretty seaside town that attracts many visitors, with a fortified tower complex and plenty of hiking opportunities. Stoupa is better for a family beach base; Kardamyli for more character.

🏖 Day 2 — Sat 27 Sep · Beach day, Mani Peninsula
No major driving — base yourself and explore locally by car (short hops 5–30 min)

Beach options — all within easy reach of Stoupa/Kardamyli:
· Kalogria/Stoupa Beach — fine sand and crystal-clear waters, ideal for swimming and sunbathing, surrounded by shops, restaurants and bars — the easiest family beach
· Kalamitsi Beach — long white sand, crystal-clear water, relaxing atmosphere — Blue Flag award — great for kids, shallow and calm
· Foneas Beach — pebbly, with deep waters, small caves and a canteen — dramatic cove, great for snorkelling
· Delfinia Beach — hidden gem between Stoupa and Kardamyli, perpetually calm crystal-clear waters, shielded from wind

Afternoon: drive south (~25 min) to Limeni — an idyllic and much-photographed bay lined with traditional Maniot buildings and stylish restaurants overlooking crystal-clear turquoise water — perfect for a late lunch or sunset dinner.
Optional: Gerolimenas (~45 min south of Stoupa) — a tiny remote fishing village at the bottom of the world, with a sheltered pebbly beach and fish tavernas right on the water.

🏰 Day 3 — Sun 28 Sep · Stoupa → Diros Caves → Monemvasia (beachside stay)
Drive time: Stoupa → Diros Caves ~45 min · Diros → Monemvasia ~2h · Total: ~2h 45m

Morning: stop at the Caves of Diros (~45 min south of Stoupa, near Areopoli) — an underground lake explored by boat, one of the most extraordinary natural sites in Greece. Book ahead.
Then drive east to Monemvasia via Areopoli and Gythio (coastal route, ~2h from Diros). Stop at Gythio for lunch on the seafront — pretty harbour town, about halfway.
Overnight in Monemvasia — the medieval fortress-town rises from the sea on a single rock connected to the mainland by a narrow causeway. Walk the old town's cobbled lanes, Byzantine churches, and eat at a terrace restaurant looking out to the Aegean. Swimming below the rock walls of the kastro is possible and memorable.

🌊 Day 4 — Mon 29 Sep · Monemvasia → Xylokastro (Gulf of Corinth)
Drive time: ~3h 30–4h via Sparta or inland route

Take a morning walk in Monemvasia before the day-trippers arrive (worth the early start), then head north. Route options: via Sparta (inland, good roads, pass by Mystras briefly) or via the east coast (more scenic, slower). Xylokastro sits on the Gulf of Corinth — a relaxed seaside town popular with Athenians — Blue Flag beaches, pine-forest coastline, calm gulf waters.
Visit a family friend — stay the night here. Easy, slow evening after a big driving day.

🏠 Day 5 — Tue 30 Sep · Xylokastro → Chalandri · Wedding prep
Drive time: ~1h 30m · ~120km via coastal road and Corinth motorway

Relaxed morning in Xylokastro, then easy drive back. Stop at the Corinth Canal — worth 20 min to look down at the extraordinary cut — then back to Chalandri. Freshen up, wedding prep, family gatherings in Halandri. Rest up — big day tomorrow.
