🚆 Getting to Marlin Apartments (London Bridge):
Train: Thameslink, direct, no changes needed. ~30–40 min journey, 39km.
Where to board: Gatwick Airport station is directly below the South Terminal — well signposted from arrivals, just follow "Trains" signs.
Tickets: No need to pre-book — just tap a contactless card or phone at the gates at Gatwick, then tap out at London Bridge. Roughly £11–17 per person one-way if buying on the day (~£55–85 for the 5 of you); cheaper if booked online in advance.
Seats: No reservations on Thameslink — just board and sit anywhere. Worth boarding a little early to get 5 seats together given you'll have luggage after a 23h+ flight.
Arrival: Trains arrive at London Bridge platforms 4 & 5. From there it's a 5–10 min walk to Empire Square / Marlin Apartments.

Arriving Marlin Apartments ~21:30. 24hr front desk — no rush.
