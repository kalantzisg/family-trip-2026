🧳
Packing & comfort tips — whole family
Long-haul, sightseeing, road trips — keeping everyone comfortable
Prep before departure
✈️ Flying pillows
Pack a neck pillow for each person — 23h+ flights are long. Inflatable ones pack flat. Kids especially benefit from head support for sleeping.
📚 Reading
Load up Kindles or tablets with books before departure — in-flight and beach reading. Each kid picks 2–3 books for the trip. Real books for the beach add weight so digital is better.
🎮 Games
Pack a compact card game (Uno, Phase 10, Exploding Kittens) and one travel board game — great for beach taverna waits, long drives, and evenings. No power needed.
📵 Minimal devices
Agree on a family device policy before the trip — especially for Greece. Screens down at meals and sightseeing. One designated photography phone per outing keeps things lighter and more present.
🎧 Headphones
Over-ear noise-cancelling for adults (long flights). In-ears for kids. Download playlists, podcasts and shows offline before departing MEL.
🌞 Sun protection
Greece in Sep/Oct still 27–32°C. Pack high SPF for everyone — especially the beach days in Stoupa and Lefkada. Hats and rashies for the kids.
💊 Health kit
Paracetamol, antihistamine, seasickness tabs (ferry/boat in Greece), blister plasters, antiseptic cream, rehydration sachets. Small kit per bag.
🔌 Power adapters
UK uses Type G plugs · Greece uses Type C/F. Pack 2× UK adapters and 2× EU adapters. A multi-port USB charger in each bedroom saves outlet fights.
🧴 Toiletries
TSA-approved liquids for carry-on (100ml). Check hold bag limits — Emirates Saver allows 2×23kg checked bags per adult.
🗂 Documents
Each adult carries a physical copy of passports, insurance policy, booking confirmations. Kids' passports stay with one adult. Digital backups on phone and cloud.
🚗 Road trip
For the Peloponnese and Lefkada drives — download Google Maps offline for Greece before leaving Marousi. Pack snacks and water in the car. Kids' headphones charged.

Option: Stop in Xylokastro on the way — Xylokastro sits right on this route on the Gulf of Corinth, ~1.5h from Chalandri. Worth stopping to visit a family friend here and potentially staying 1–2 nights before continuing to Lefkada. Blue Flag beaches, pine-forest coastline, calm gulf waters — a natural decompression stop post-wedding. Adds 1–2 days before the Vassiliki beach leg.

Base: Vassiliki, southern Lefkada — 3 nights
Lefkada's most picturesque fishing village — perfect for families, shallow warm waters, cafes and restaurants right on the beach. Late September/early October the crowds are gone, the water is still warm, and the pace drops right down. Pick a spot and zone out.
